## Full description

ReaxKey brings your own stickers into a familiar Android keyboard. Type normally, then open the Media panel to find a reaction and send it in a compatible app.

Add stickers with Android's document picker, share them to ReaxKey, or connect a folder you choose. ReaxKey can also look for accessible shared sticker sources when you allow it. Android may keep another app's private media out of reach, so discovery varies by phone and source.

Browse and search your local sticker library, mark favorites and organize collections. A companion app lets you manage sources and choose light or dark appearance. Your index stays on your device. ReaxKey needs no account, ads, subscription or Internet permission.

Rich-content sending depends on the receiving app and field. Telegram and Snapchat have been reported working in physical owner tests; exact versions and formats have not yet been recorded. Other messaging apps are not verified. If a field cannot accept a sticker from the keyboard, ReaxKey explains that and offers an explicit share option.

The app is planned as a one-time paid download; pricing is set in Google Play.
