# Play Console handoff checklist

No account, listing, legal declaration or live price has been changed by the agent. Complete and verify these in the owner's Play Console before any submission:

- Developer account identity/verification and any applicable closed-testing requirement.
- Payments profile, tax/banking information and paid-app setup.
- Permanent package identity `com.reaxkey.keyboard`; Play App Signing and safeguarded upload key.
- Signed `1.0.0` AAB with a never-before-used positive version code.
- App name, descriptions, category, support email, publisher identity, privacy-policy HTTPS URL, genuine screenshots, feature graphic and Play icon.
- Ads: **No** for the current binary. Account requirement: **No**. App access instructions: keyboard activation through Android settings; no login.
- Target audience and content-rating questionnaire answered for actual content/experience, without invented ratings.
- Data Safety form checked against final bundle and `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY_DRAFT.md`.
- Photo/video permission declaration for `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`; resolve any Play rejection before rollout.
- Countries/regions and one-time price; US plan $2.99, Nigeria target about ₦2,999, subject to Play localization and owner decision.
- Internal/closed test track acquisition, install, update, grant persistence and required tester eligibility.
- Production release review, legal agreements and final rollout controlled by owner.

Google Play requires new phone/tablet apps submitted after 2026-08-31 to target API 36 or higher. The current app targets API 37. See Google's [target API policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878) and [photo/video permission policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16558241) at submission time.
