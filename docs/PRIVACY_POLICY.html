ReaxKey Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026. Publisher/contact: The publisher must add its legal name and support email before this page is published or used for a Play listing.

ReaxKey is an Android keyboard and companion app for finding and using your own stickers. It receives the characters you type into the active app and sends them to that app through Android's input method interface. ReaxKey does not save typed messages or passwords, send them to a ReaxKey server, or use them for analytics or advertising. Secure fields disable usage-learning behavior.

Sticker library and local data

ReaxKey stores a local index of stickers you import or grant access to. Records may include content URI, source, file name, format, dimensions, labels and tags, favorites, collections, use count and last-used time. Source URI/grant and health information and keyboard preferences are also stored locally. Imported sticker copies are kept in private app storage. Conversation text and recipients are not recorded as part of sticker usage.

Optional Android image permission helps discover shared stickers automatically. Android's selected-media option is supported where available. You can decline this access and use Android's document picker to select stickers or a folder, or share stickers into ReaxKey. Folder access requires your approval in Android's picker and can persist until revoked. ReaxKey cannot access every private app folder and does not request all-files access.

When you explicitly send a sticker, ReaxKey grants the destination app temporary read access to its content URI. That app's policy governs how it handles the sticker. Not every app accepts rich content.

Network, diagnostics and third parties

The current app declares no Internet permission and has no account, cloud sync, ads, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or ReaxKey backend. ReaxKey does not transmit local library data or typed text to itself. Android system services, the app store and destination apps operate under their own terms.

An optional diagnostics export saves a text file where you choose. It contains app version, Android API, manufacturer, indexed-item count and aggregate source-health counts; it excludes typed messages, sticker bytes and recipients. You choose whether to share it with support.

Retention and choices

Records and imported copies stay on your device until removed or until you uninstall or clear ReaxKey storage. You can disconnect a source, remove an item, revoke permission in Android settings, or clear storage. Disconnecting a source hides its indexed items and does not delete original media. Android backup and device transfer of ReaxKey private data are disabled. Copies sent to another app are controlled by that app.

Normal typing does not require media access. You can change keyboard selection and permissions in Android settings. For privacy questions or deletion help, contact the support address on the published Play listing once provided by the publisher.

This policy must be reviewed against the final signed binary and updated if permissions, SDKs or data handling change.
