# ReaxKey Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-02. **Publisher/contact:** The publisher must add its legal name and support email before posting this policy or submitting a Play listing.

ReaxKey is an Android keyboard and a companion app for finding and using your own stickers. A keyboard can receive the characters you type into the app where it is active. ReaxKey sends those characters to that app through Android's input method interface. ReaxKey does not save typed messages or passwords, send them to a ReaxKey server, or use them for analytics or advertising. Secure fields disable usage-learning behavior.

## Sticker library and local data

ReaxKey stores a local index of stickers you choose to import or grant access to. The index may contain a sticker's content URI, source, file name, format, dimensions, labels and tags, favorites, collections, use count and last-used time. It stores source URI/grant and health information and keyboard preferences. The database is in ReaxKey's private app storage. Imported sticker copies are also stored there. ReaxKey does not record conversation text or recipients as part of sticker usage.

To find shared stickers automatically, you may choose to grant Android image access. On Android 13 and newer this uses image/media permissions, including Android's selected-media option where available; on older supported versions it uses read access to shared storage. You can decline this optional access and still use Android's document picker to choose stickers or a folder, or share stickers into ReaxKey. A folder grant is approved in Android's picker and can persist until revoked. Android and other apps may prevent ReaxKey from accessing private sticker folders. The app does not use all-files access.

When you explicitly send a sticker, ReaxKey gives the destination app temporary read access to that sticker through Android's content-URI sharing mechanism. The destination app's own privacy policy then governs its handling of the sticker. ReaxKey cannot promise that every app accepts rich content.

## Network, diagnostics and third parties

The current ReaxKey app declares no Internet permission and has no account, cloud sync, ads, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or ReaxKey backend. Local library data and typed text are not transmitted to ReaxKey. Android system services, the app store and destination messaging apps operate under their own terms.

An optional, user-initiated diagnostics export writes a text file to the location you choose. It includes app version, Android API, manufacturer, indexed-item count and aggregate source-health counts. It does not include typed messages, sticker bytes or chat recipients. You decide whether to share that file with support.

## Retention and choices

Library records and imported copies remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall ReaxKey. You can disconnect a source, remove an item, revoke a folder/media permission in Android settings, or clear ReaxKey's storage. Disconnecting a source hides its indexed items; it does not delete originals in another app or folder. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for ReaxKey private data. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local index and imported copies, subject to Android's normal file handling. Copies you already sent to another app are controlled by that app.

You can use normal typing without granting media access. You can change ReaxKey's keyboard selection and permissions in Android settings. For privacy questions or deletion help, contact the support address shown in the published Play listing once the publisher has provided it.

This policy must be re-reviewed against the final signed binary and updated if the app's permissions, SDKs or data handling change.
